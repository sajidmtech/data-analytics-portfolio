# IBM HR Analytics — Employee Compensation & Performance

## Overview
Analysis of 1,470 IBM employees across three business questions 
using a real world Kaggle dataset to identify compensation 
fairness, overtime attrition risk, and performance distribution.

## Tools Used
- MySQL / DataGrip — data querying and analysis
- Excel — summary tables, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIF, 
  conditional formatting
- Power BI — single page dashboard with KPI cards, 
  DAX measures and reference lines

## Business Questions
1. Which departments have the highest performing employees?
2. Does overtime affect attrition rates?
3. Are high performers compensated fairly?

## Key Findings
- High performers earn $223 less per month than average 
  performers despite receiving 56% larger salary hikes 
  (21.85% vs 14%) — pointing to a starting salary problem
- Overtime workers leave at 3x the rate of non-overtime 
  workers (30.5% vs 10.4%) with an estimated $3.5M 
  replacement cost impact
- R&D accounts for 65% of all high performers with the 
  highest concentration of high performing job roles
