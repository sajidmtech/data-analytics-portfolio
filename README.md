### 2. IBM HR Analytics — Employee Compensation & Performance
Analysis of 1,470 IBM employee records investigating 
compensation fairness, overtime attrition, and performance 
distribution across departments.

**Key findings:**
- High performers earn $223 less per month than average 
  performers despite receiving 56% larger salary hikes
- Overtime workers leave at 3x the rate of non-overtime 
  workers (30.5% vs 10.4%) — $3.5M estimated replacement cost
- R&D accounts for 65% of all high performers

**Tools:** MySQL · Power BI · Excel
